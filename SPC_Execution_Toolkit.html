01 · Strategic priorities

Six bets we are building this toolkit around

These bets shape every decision — what we say yes to, what we say no to, and where the small team spends scarce hours.

Priority 1

Build the email list as the #1 owned asset

Email is the highest-leverage channel for small nonprofits and the most preferred update channel for donors. We move email capture from a footer afterthought to the central call-to-action across web, social bios, on-park signage, and partner co-promotion.

Source: Nonprofit Tech for Good — 2026 Email Marketing Statistics — "48% of donors cite email as their preferred method of hearing updates and appeals."

Priority 2

Position Sequoia as the "other California"

LA28 will saturate the LA-coast-Hollywood image of California. SPC's strategic gift is the counter-image: ancient, vast, quiet, awe-scaled. Every piece of LA28-era content should make Sequoia feel like the missing half of a California trip, not a substitute for the city.

Priority 3

Lead with the near-loss / wildfire recovery story

The KNP Complex (2021) and the 2020 Castle Fire killed a meaningful share of the world's mature giant sequoias and reframed what "saving" the park means. This is SPC's most distinctive emotional story — visitor and donor framing should move from generic "preserve nature" to "we nearly lost these trees, and we're still in recovery."

Source: NPS — Giant Sequoias and Fire (Sequoia & Kings Canyon).

Priority 4

Open a B2B club & group logistics lane

USC Peaks, UCLA outdoor programs, Sierra Club chapters, REI Outdoor School cohorts, corporate offsite planners. A simple "Sequoia Group Trip Planner" PDF plus a group inquiry form turns informal interest into a measurable funnel without new hires.

Priority 5

Stand up an LA28 landing page early

One persistent URL (e.g. sequoiaparksconservancy.org/la28) — not a pop-up campaign — that collects search traffic, hosts the "LA to Sequoia" itinerary, hosts the email signup, and becomes the link target for every Olympic-era partner activation through July 2028.

Priority 6

Define a Minimum Viable Activation

Every new partner conversation should map to a default lightweight activation we can ship in <30 days with no new tools: a co-branded itinerary post, a Reel collab, a Constant Contact co-send, or a Field Institute group day. Anything heavier is opt-in, not the default ask.

02 · Audiences

Three working segments — not a fixed framing

These segments are working hypotheses to focus near-term effort. Expect them to be refined as Constant Contact engagement, Google Analytics, and the in-development Power BI dashboard deliver real behavioral data.

Segment A · Reach & credibility

USC & the Olympic athlete network

USC Olympic alumni, current student-athletes, LA28-affiliated networks, and high-visibility individuals with strong personal brands.

Why this matters

  • USC is one of the strongest Olympic pipelines in the U.S. and structurally embedded in LA28.
  • Highest credibility ceiling per hour of work; lowest mass-participation yield.

How we activate

  • Athlete Ambassador Program (retired Olympians and LA28 participants) for social takeovers.
  • USC Athletics & alumni-association engagement with auction-grade Sequoia experiences.
  • Storytelling, not sponsorship — coordinated with Learfield to avoid rights conflicts.

Recommendation: focus on credibility and high-value relationships, not volume.

Segment B · Action & participation

SoCal outdoor & active-lifestyle community

Hikers, climbers, trail runners, university recreation groups, and digitally engaged Strava-style users in LA and the broader Southern California metro.

Why this matters

  • Already self-selected toward outdoor recreation — lowest barrier to repeat visitation.
  • Most likely to model stewardship behavior to others.

How we activate

  • Outdoor club partnerships: Sierra Club Angeles chapter, USC Peaks, UCLA outdoor programs.
  • Co-branded weekend trips with planning resources and stewardship guidelines.
  • Strava / AllTrails branded challenges Suggested future capability — partnership agreement and budget required.

Recommendation: focus on action, repeat visits, and stewardship adoption.

Segment C · Reach & positioning

Brands, creators & Olympic-era travelers

Outdoor and athletic creators, fitness/wellness brands, and domestic and international visitors traveling to Los Angeles for LA28.

Why this matters

  • Bridges tourism, lifestyle, and outdoor recreation in one narrative arc.
  • Positions Sequoia inside a broader "California experience" travel story.

How we activate

  • Direct creator outreach (outdoor, wellness, athlete lifestyle); leverage existing supporters such as Martin Henderson.
  • "LA to Sequoia" 4-day road-trip itinerary as the flagship landing-page asset.
  • Distribution via tourism platforms, hotel concierge programs, rental car partners.

Recommendation: focus on awareness and integration into broader travel narratives.

How we use these in practice

Working segments, not a rigid framework

These three segments are a starting hypothesis to focus the first 6–9 months of execution. As Constant Contact list segmentation, Google Analytics behavior data, and the in-development Power BI dashboard mature, expect 1–2 of these to split (e.g. SoCal outdoor splitting into "weekend trail user" vs. "club / group leader") and at least one new segment to emerge from donor data.

Each segment maps to a different KPI: A → reach & credibility metrics, B → visit / repeat-visit metrics, C → site sessions & itinerary downloads.

Open with USC Athletics' external-affairs leadership (oversees marketing, comms, branding, partnerships). Frame: civic storytelling and tourism partner tied to LA28, not a commercial sponsor. Explicitly state non-conflict with existing athletics sponsorship.

After alignment, coordinate with the Learfield-side leadership overseeing sponsorships, NIL, multimedia rights, and corporate partnerships to define clear boundaries for SPC's involvement and identify permissible collaboration zones.

The strategic gift to USC: an authentic, behind-the-scenes Olympic narrative produced collaboratively rather than sold. The strategic gift to SPC: cultural relevance, donor visibility, and a credible non-commercial bridge into Olympic audiences.

05 · Visitor-to-donor funnel

Strengthen every link, lead with email capture

Visitors are more likely to donate when they understand where the money goes and feel their personal contribution mattered. This funnel mirrors the SPC donor-funnel proposal and adds the email-list-first emphasis.

1

Awareness

What does SPC do, and why does this matter? Visitor and LA-tourist messaging on the landing page, social channels, and gateway-city signage.

2

Emotional connection

I care about this place. Embedded storytelling on trails, near-loss / wildfire framing, signs at landmark sites (General Sherman, Moro Rock, Tunnel Log, Tokopah Falls).

3

Tangible impact

Where does my money go? Split donations into tracks (trail maintenance, wildlife recovery, replanting). Anchor with low entry points: "$5 helps maintain 5 feet of trail."

4

Retention

I can feel the impact. Personalized follow-ups via Constant Contact: thank-you, before/after photos, recovery progress, low-friction option to give again.

Landmark moments

Place donation prompts at scenic spots where emotional connection peaks. QR signage: "Scan to learn about this place — and help keep it standing." Higher-traffic landmarks first.

Mobile-friendly donating

Donation pages must support Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Venmo (or equivalent low-friction wallets) to minimize abandoned giving on phones.

Email capture comes first

Where it's a choice between a donation prompt and an email capture, capture the email. Email retention compounds; one-time donations don't. Donor retention increases by 29% for offline donors when you have their email address. Source: Nonprofit Tech for Good citing NextAfter.

06 · Content & short-form social

Small-team TikTok & Reels strategy

Built for a 1–2 person execution team. Volume comes from re-cutting one shoot day into many surfaces, not from being everywhere. Canva is the default design surface; Adobe is the deeper-edit tool when needed.

Content pillars (rotate)

  • The giants up close — awe-scale moments at Sherman, Moro, Tokopah.
  • Near-loss / recovery — wildfire stories, replanting, science updates.
  • "Other California" — LA28-era counter-image content for travelers.
  • Behind the work — trail crew, scientists, junior rangers.
  • Stewardship & how to visit well — Leave No Trace, accessibility.

Cadence (sustainable)

  • 3 short-form / week across TikTok and Instagram Reels (post once, cross-post via Later.com).
  • 1 carousel / week on Instagram or LinkedIn (data, behind-the-scenes, donor impact).
  • 1 longer cut / month on YouTube (re-edit of the best short-form moment).
  • 1 Reddit post / month in r/NationalParks or r/CaliforniaHiking, value-first not promotional.

Production model

  • One shoot day = ~12 short-form posts. Always overshoot B-roll.
  • Phone-first capture is fine; vertical 9:16 by default.
  • Caption hook in the first 1.5 seconds; voiceover scripted but conversational.
  • Bit.ly every CTA link to track click-through outside of GA.

10 short-form formats that travel

  1. "Things I wish I knew before visiting Sequoia."
  2. "This tree is 2,200+ years old. Here's what it has lived through."
  3. "Stuff you can only see in Sequoia, not Yosemite."
  4. "3 stops between LA and the General Sherman Tree."
  5. "Behind a single day of trail crew work."
  6. "How a giant sequoia survives a fire."
  7. "What we lost in the 2020/2021 fires — in numbers."
  8. "Ask the ranger" rapid-fire Q&A.
  9. Junior Ranger badge unboxing and what's funded by SPC.
  10. "Quiet park" ASMR / sound-first edits for sleep / focus audiences.

Channel ownership

  • Instagram & TikTok — primary acquisition; identical Reels with platform-native captions.
  • LinkedIn — B2B groups, donors, partner discovery; corporate-side voice.
  • YouTube — long-tail SEO and landing-page embeds; weekly is unrealistic, monthly is fine.
  • Facebook — older and family donor audience; recycle Reels and event listings.
  • Reddit — trip-planning audience; show up as a useful park, not a brand.
  • LinkTree — the mandatory funnel surface from every social bio → LA28 page, donate, group inquiry, email signup.

1. Short-form social caption (TikTok / Reels)

Works in: Claude · ChatGPT · Gemini 
You are a brand voice writer for Sequoia Parks Conservancy (SPC), a nonprofit
supporting Sequoia & Kings Canyon National Parks. Write a short-form social
caption for a [TikTok / Instagram Reel] about [TOPIC OR FOOTAGE DESCRIPTION].

Voice: awe-forward, factual, no exaggeration, no clickbait, no exclamation
spam. Lead with a 1-line hook a viewer would stop scrolling for. Maximum
120 words. End with one clear CTA, choosing only from this list:
- "Tap the LinkTree to plan a visit"
- "Tap the LinkTree to support wildfire recovery"
- "Tap the LinkTree to subscribe for park updates"
- "Comment a question and a ranger will answer this week"

Hard rules:
- Do NOT invent visitor numbers, donor numbers, or partner relationships.
- Do NOT claim official sponsor / official partner status with any brand.
- If you reference a stat, use only stats I provide below; otherwise stay
  qualitative.
- Reference giant sequoias, fire recovery, or "the other California"
  framing if it fits naturally; do not force them.

Approved facts (use only these if you cite anything):
- General Sherman is the largest living single-stem tree on Earth by volume.
- The 2020 Castle Fire and 2021 KNP Complex fires killed a meaningful share
  of mature giant sequoias; recovery and replanting are ongoing (NPS).
- LA28 Olympic Opening Ceremony is July 14, 2028 (la28.org).

Output: 3 caption options. Each labeled A/B/C with a 1-line "why this hook
works" note for the team.
